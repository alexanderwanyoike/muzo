# Muzo 0.0.5

Personalise Muzo with a new material and six glowing accent colours.

## What changed

- Pulse adds a flat, layered material inspired by Spotify, with light and dark variants.
- Neutral hover surfaces and clear keyboard focus rings keep Pulse controls readable without turning every interaction into an accent colour.
- Choose Glow green, cyan, blue, violet, pink or orange in Settings under Appearance. Classic accents and your current appearance remain available.
- Glowing controls use separate foreground colours for readable text, icons and focus indicators across materials. Glow effects are removed in system high contrast mode.

## Updating

Muzo checks for updates at startup and every six hours. In an installed Linux x64 AppImage or Windows x64 build, click Update available v0.0.5 at the bottom of the left sidebar to download. When it is ready, choose Restart to install and confirm. Playback stops while Muzo installs the update and reopens.

If you want to check immediately after this release is published, close Muzo and reopen your installed app. Development builds do not check for updates.

AppImage updates keep the existing filename and location, so menu entries and dock pins keep working. Your libraries, playlists, cached artwork, Dropbox credentials and appearance settings continue to use the existing Muzo profile.

The release includes a Linux x64 AppImage, a Windows x64 installer, macOS installers for Apple silicon and Intel, SHA256SUMS, a distribution manifest and Windows/Linux update metadata. These preview builds are unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized. macOS update notices open a manual installer download.
