# Muzo 0.0.6

Updates now live in a quiet application status bar.

## What changed

- A slim footer spans the full library window, with a small text pill at the bottom right.
- The update icon and two-line sidebar notice are replaced by a single line showing checking, an available version, download percentage, restart readiness or a retryable error.
- Click Up to date to check again. Download and restart remain explicit actions, with confirmation before playback stops.
- The footer follows your material and light/dark appearance. Keyboard focus stays visible and the footer stays out of the mini player.

## Updating

If you are running 0.0.5, use its existing notice at the bottom of the sidebar to download 0.0.6, then choose Restart to install and confirm. After this update, future notices appear at the bottom right in the application status bar.

Muzo checks at startup and every six hours. Close and reopen your installed app to check immediately. You can keep listening while an update downloads.

Linux x64 AppImages and Windows x64 installers support in-place updates. AppImages keep their filename and location, so menu entries and dock pins still point to the same file. Your libraries, playlists, artwork cache, appearance and Dropbox credentials continue to use the existing Muzo profile.

The release includes Linux x64, Windows x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel downloads, SHA256SUMS, a distribution manifest and Windows/Linux update metadata. These preview builds are unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized. macOS updates continue through a manual installer download.
