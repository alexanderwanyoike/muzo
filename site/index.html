Made for listening

Keep your collection
close at hand.

A familiar desktop layout, with your sources beside your music and playback always within reach.

01

From a folder to a library.

Add local folders or browse Dropbox to choose one or more locations. Subfolders are included, and your libraries stay in sync with their sources.

02

Find it. Queue it. Settle in.

Search your sources, resize the sidebar and make playlists for whatever comes next. Previous and next controls follow your playlist queue.

03

A familiar cover, every time.

Embedded album art belongs alongside your tracks and in the current sleeve. A persistent local cache keeps previously loaded artwork ready between sessions.
